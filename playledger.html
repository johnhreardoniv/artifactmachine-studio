What it does

  • Tonight picks one game to return to and one to try, and says why.
  • Time adds up your hours by type of game, for your whole library and for each week from the day you start it. Click a type to see each game in it and how it was classified.
  • Insights shows how your taste is moving, what you leave early, what you buy and don't start, and the studios you keep returning to.
  • Next up ranks your backlog, wishlist and other Steam games by how you've actually spent your time, and names the tags and games behind each pick. No AI model is involved.
  • Revisit finds the games you put serious hours into and stopped, and the ones you barely tried.
  • News and Releases follow the games you own and the ones you're waiting for.
  • Connections connects Steam, GOG Galaxy, Epic and Playnite from one page, with numbered setup steps for each.

What it reads

Playledger runs on your PC and keeps your data there, with no account and nothing to sign up for. It reads the files Steam keeps on your computer. It also asks Steam's public store and news services about your games, so Steam sees those requests. It talks to no other service, it can't change anything in Steam, and it can't log in to your account.

If you use other stores, it also reads what the Epic launcher and GOG Galaxy keep on your PC, and a file you export from Playnite. Either can also bring in Xbox, Battle.net, Ubisoft and others: GOG Galaxy for stores you've linked inside it, Playnite for its library add-ons. It never signs in to them, and it looks each title up on Steam to match it. Those games join your recommendations, and their hours stay out of your Steam time. GOG Galaxy's file has been read on one real PC; the Epic launcher's files and Playnite's export only on sample files.

A free Steam Web API key is optional. It adds achievements and any games missing from those local files.

Start it

  1. Unzip the download somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder. You'll need Steam installed and 64-bit Windows.
  2. Double-click Playledger.exe. Your browser opens the app, and a small window stays open while it runs.
  3. To have it start quietly each time you sign in, open a Command Prompt in that folder and run Playledger.exe autostart on. Weekly hours only count while it's running.

Why Windows warns you

This program isn't code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". If you trust where you got the link, choose "More info", then "Run anyway". To confirm your copy is the published one, run certutil -hashfile playledger-0.5.0-windows-x64.zip SHA256 and compare the result with this:

51941a55fab5f01b876b30816ae2ac60b6f6b94dcfbc4fd5c2ecd51259550515

Mac and Linux

The Mac and Linux download (0.4 MB) runs on your own copy of Node.js, version 22.13 or newer. Unpack it somewhere permanent and run node playledger.cjs in that folder.

It hasn't been tried on a Mac yet. On Linux it has only been tried in a container with sample Steam files, not with Steam itself. On both, Playledger can't see which game is running, so your hours update after you stop playing and Steam saves them, with no live sessions. Starting it at sign-in is up to you, and the README shows the command.

To confirm your copy, run sha256sum on Linux or shasum -a 256 on a Mac, and compare the result with this:

Release history

The buttons at the top of this page always download the newest version. To update, download it and replace the old program. Your data stays where it is.

Version 0.5.0 Latest

November 6, 2026

  • Stores is now Connections: every store connects on one page, with numbered setup steps for each one, and the green button points at the next one to connect. The Steam key moved here from Settings.
  • Far more games from other stores find their Steam match. The rule hasn't loosened: one exact match on Steam, or none.
  • Games from GOG Galaxy bring their genres, critics' scores and purchase dates, and game pages show the hours and achievements other stores have for them. Connections shows how much each store filled in.
  • A "Hide adult titles" setting, off by default. It keeps them out of suggestions and releases, never out of games you own.
  • The sidebar folds from a small round button on its edge.
  • Fixed: one game Steam failed to answer for could stop achievements updating for every game.
