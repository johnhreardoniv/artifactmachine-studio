Search engines found you. Answer engines can’t read you.

We measure whether a machine sent to answer a question about a company can actually read its website — and whether that company has been paying for search visibility it no longer gets. One page, two seconds, no account.

What we won’t tell you

Whether an AI assistant mentions you today. That needs repeated sampling of the models themselves, several companies sell it, and this is not it. This is the cheaper question underneath: if a model does come looking, is there anything here it can use?

We also won’t claim the gap is a signal nobody else has. Across 3,000 randomly sampled domains it correlates 0.84 with search readiness alone — a genuinely different ranking, about half the top 200 differs, but not an independent one. That number is published in the study rather than left out of it.
