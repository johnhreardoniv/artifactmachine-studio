02 / Process

Ambiguity is engineered, not accidental.

Anyone can make a thing that does nothing. Making a thing that looks like it must do something, and then making sure it does not, takes discipline.

  1. Stage 01

    Conception

    Every object begins as a real, useful product. Our design team then removes the purpose, one feature at a time, until only the sense of a purpose remains.

  2. Stage 02

    Manufacture

    Objects are produced to tolerances of ±0.01 mm, because a vague object made carelessly is just rubbish. Ours are made carefully, which is why nobody throws them away.

  3. Stage 03

    The Identification Panel

    Every unit is shown to forty members of the public. If any one of them can say what it is for, the unit is destroyed. Our rejection rate is 31%, and we are proud of every rejection.

  4. Stage 04

    Distribution

    Our objects are not sold. They are found. Each one arrives in its drawer the way these things always have: nobody saw it happen, and nobody can say when.
